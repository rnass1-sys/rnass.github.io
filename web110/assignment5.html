Activity 1: Social Networking Tutorial (TutorialsPoint) This activity focused on understanding the fundamentals of social networking and how social media platforms function within Web 2.0 environments. I learned that social media lets users create and share content while interacting with others through online communities. A key concept I gained was that user-generated content is the foundation of social media, and platforms are designed to connect individuals through profiles, communication, and shared interests. Social media also operates differently from traditional media because it allows many users to communicate with many others at the same time rather than one source delivering information to a large audience. This understanding can help me use social platforms more effectively for communication and collaboration in my current and future environments. In web design and interactive media, I can use this knowledge to create user-centered platforms that encourage interaction and engagement.

Activity 2: Blogging and Content Creation The blogging activity introduced blogs as a form of social media where users can share information, opinions, and experiences. I learned that blogs are structured as ongoing posts that allow for interaction through comments and sharing, making them an important tool for communication and personal branding. Blogs are also an example of user-generated content, which plays a major role in social media environments. Additionally, blogging helps individuals organize ideas and present information clearly to an audience. I can apply this knowledge in my current and future environment by creating a blog to showcase my work in web design and interactive media. It can serve as a portfolio where I share projects, reflect on my learning, and communicate ideas professionally while building an online presence.

Activity 3: Social Media Platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube) This activity focused on analyzing different social media platforms and their unique purposes. I learned that each platform supports different types of content and interactions. For example, Instagram emphasizes photo and video sharing, LinkedIn focuses on professional networking and résumé sharing, and YouTube centers on video content and audience engagement. These platforms all rely on user interaction and content sharing but are designed for different audiences and goals. This knowledge allows me to strategically match platforms to my objectives, using LinkedIn for professional growth and Instagram to showcase creative work. This is especially important in interactive media, where understanding audience and platform differences can improve communication and engagement.

Activity 4: Honeycomb Model Evaluation The Honeycomb Model activity helped me understand the key elements that define social media platforms, including identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. I learned that each platform emphasizes different elements depending on its purpose, which influences how users interact within the platform. This model provides a framework for analyzing and comparing social media sites based on their features and user experience. In my future environment, I can apply this concept when designing websites or digital platforms by focusing on specific elements that best meet user needs. For instance, I can prioritize communication features for community building based on the platform's objective. This understanding will help me create more effective and engaging digital experiences in web design and interactive media.
