What I Learned

While completing these activities, I explored the differences between raster and vector graphics and learned how to incorporate various multimedia types into a website. Raster graphics are made up of individual pixels and are commonly used for photographs and detailed images. In contrast, vector graphics rely on mathematical paths, lines, and shapes, enabling them to scale infinitely without losing resolution. I also gained a better understanding of how HTML elements, including image, audio, video, and iframe tags, are used to display multimedia content on webpages. These activities strengthened my hands-on skills in graphic creation and web design. I can apply what I learned to my current and future web development projects by selecting appropriate image formats, incorporating audio and video, and creating webpages that are visually appealing, organized, and interactive for visitors.
