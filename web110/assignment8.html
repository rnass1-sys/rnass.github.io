Assignment 8

Activity 1: Internet Safety and Internet Security Overview

The Internet Safety series from LearnFree.org and the Internet Security Overview from TutorialsPoint helped me understand the importance of protecting personal information while using the internet. I learned that common threats include phishing, malware, spam, hacking, and identity theft. Phishing is especially dangerous because scammers can create fake emails and websites that look legitimate to trick people into sharing passwords or financial information. I also learned that internet security helps protect the privacy and accuracy of information shared online. I can apply this knowledge by avoiding suspicious links, verifying websites before entering personal information, and being careful about what I share on social media. These habits will help me protect my information in school, at home, and in a future workplace.

Activity 2: Practice Web Browser Safety

The web browser safety activity focused on online privacy and safer browsing habits. I learned that browser updates help fix security weaknesses and that cookies and browsing history can contain information about my online activities. Private browsing can limit the information saved on my device, but it does not make me completely anonymous online. I also learned the importance of checking website addresses before entering sensitive information. I can apply these concepts by keeping my browser updated, reviewing privacy settings, and avoiding unfamiliar websites that request personal information. These practices can help me browse more safely while completing assignments, researching information, and using online services.

Activity 3: Anti-Malware Software

The anti-malware activity demonstrated how security software safeguards computers from harmful programs. I learned that malware can damage files, steal personal information, or prevent someone from accessing their own data. While security software detects and eliminates many threats, it cannot guarantee immunity against all attacks. Regular updates and responsible internet use also help protect a device. I can apply this knowledge by keeping security software updated, scanning my computer regularly, and downloading files only from trustworthy sources. This will help protect my school assignments, personal documents, and other important files.

Activity 4: Password Managers and Multi-Factor Authentication

The password manager and multi-factor authentication activity helped me understand how to secure accounts. I learned that reusing passwords across multiple accounts increases the risk of someone accessing several accounts if one password is stolen. Password managers can help create and store unique passwords, while multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection by requiring additional verification. I can apply these concepts by using different, strong passwords for my email and social media accounts and enabling multi-factor authentication whenever it is available. These habits will help protect my identity and personal information as I continue using technology for school, work, and everyday activities.
