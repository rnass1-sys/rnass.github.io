One activity I completed was “Compare Search Engines.” It focused on evaluating different search engines such as Google, Bing, and Yahoo based on their results and user interfaces. From this activity, I learned that while all search engines are designed to locate information on the web, they differ in how they rank results and present information to users. The effectiveness of a search engine depends largely on the relevance of the results it provides, which is determined by algorithms and indexing systems. I also noticed that some search engines may prioritize certain results differently, which can impact the overall usefulness of the search. I can apply this knowledge in my current academic environment by choosing the most effective search engine for research tasks and comparing results across platforms to ensure I am accessing the most relevant and accurate information. Another activity I completed was “Use Search Engines.” This activity covered advanced search techniques such as exact phrase searching, Boolean operators, and site-specific searches. I learned how to refine search queries using exact phrase matching (quotation marks) and Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) to generate more precise and relevant results. These techniques significantly improve the ability to find precise, relevant information rather than broad or unrelated results. This activity also shows how refining search queries can make research more efficient. I can apply this concept in my current coursework by improving how I search for academic sources, saving time and increasing the quality of my research. In the future, especially in web design and interactive media, understanding how users search will help me design content that is easier to find and better optimized for search engines. A third activity I completed was “Learn about Search Engine Optimization (SEO).” This activity focused on how websites can improve their visibility in search engine results. I learned that SEO involves optimizing content so it appears higher in unpaid or organic search results, which increases user traffic. This includes understanding how search engines index content and how keywords and metadata influence rankings. I also learned that website owners can submit pages directly to search engines to ensure they are indexed more quickly. I can apply this knowledge in my future career in web design by creating websites that follow SEO best practices, making them more accessible and visible to users. This will be especially valuable when designing professional websites that need to attract and engage a target audience effectively.
